Previously evaluated source
---------------------------
Mellow Shader v3.3 by TheCMK was studied during the original Cubiz Lite
prototype. The later Glimmer migration removes all Mellow water and underwater
code from the active renderer. Its original MIT/Apache licenses and edit notice
remain preserved in /licenses/Mellow for complete project provenance.

Integration
-----------
Cubiz Lite adapts Glimmer's water algorithms and caustics resource to Kute's
GLSL 1.20 scene/depth pipeline. Glimmer remains the sole source of water and
underwater behavior; Mellow code and the previous Kute water renderer are not
included in this build.
