Cubiz Lite contains a modified integration of the water-related portions of
Glimmer v1.5.2.

The port retains Glimmer's procedural wave normals, optional water parallax,
absorption and scattering coefficients, Fresnel behavior, fast/SSR reflection
model, refraction approach, caustic lookup resource, and water transition
intent. These systems were adapted from Glimmer's GLSL 4.30 pipeline to Kute's
GLSL 1.20 buffers and Kute-provided lighting/sky inputs.

No Glimmer branding, icon, screenshots, or unrelated renderer systems are
included.
